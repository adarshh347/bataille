# Bataille — a working dossier

Not a museum. A toolkit. We are reading Georges Bataille (1897–1962) to find
what is still *live* — concepts that cut in 2026 — and we are throwing out the
two dead ways of reading him:

1. **The edgelord read** — Bataille as shock, transgression, "philosopher of
   evil," *Story of the Eye*. Culture has fully digested and defanged this.
2. **The seminar read** — Bataille as a node in French Theory, approached
   through Derrida's footnotes, in the dusty reverent register.

We want the third thing: Bataille as a set of *instruments* we test against the
present — climate and energy, AI compute, the attention economy, the death of
transgression, optimization culture, the search for non-instrumental experience.

## Method

- **Talk a lot.** Discussion is the engine. Cross-questioning, not summary.
- **Extract structures.** Insights that survive discussion get written down.
- **Build agentic layers.** Reusable interpretive lenses become `skills/`.
