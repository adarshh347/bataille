## suggestions — on what you can prompt better

reading what you're after, a few prompt-shape moves that would sharpen the sandbox's output for *your* axis:

1. **swap "generate an essay" for "perform a move on this passage."** the sandbox currently has a per-clause READ button that asks the model to apply a lens to a whole book. that's expensive and diffuse. a complementary cheaper mode: pick *one passage* (paste it into a field), pick *one operation* from a fixed vocabulary — `outward-project · find-kin · install-deep-version · refute · extend-one-sentence · vedic-translate · stand-where-the-author-stood` — and run. operations as prompt-primitives, not free-form. this is what i'd want a footnote-level companion to do.
