# Aim

What I'm trying to do here is treat Georges Bataille — base materialism, the accursed share, sovereignty, eroticism, inner experience, the formless — as a live thinking toolkit for 2026, not a historical artifact. Two 20th-century framings I want to leave behind: the "edgelord of transgression" reading that fetishizes the shock, and the reverent French-Theory-seminar reading that drains the blood out of him. What survives both filters is the part of Bataille that still cuts — his model of economy, sacredness, sovereignty, and the formless, applied to compute, attention, climate, eroticism-vs-algorithms, and the politics of the present.

The method is discussion-driven. Insights only get written down once they've survived a back-and-forth — I argue, the assistant cross-questions, the assistant argues, I cross-question. Nothing lands in `insights/` until it has been forged that way. Agents and PDF extraction feed `knowledge/` and `sources/` (raw maps of the books, the concept lexicon, modern-context candidates, discourse research). That material is fuel, not product. The value of the project is what comes out of conversation about that material.

In parallel, I want to build a layered agentic system around this work — multiple sub-agents doing different jobs (book extraction, concept lexicon, modern-context mapping, discourse research) while the main thread stays the discussion. The project is partly about Bataille and partly about figuring out what a smart, multi-layered, agent-assisted intellectual practice actually looks like. The two halves are meant to feed each other: Bataille's "general economy" is one of the lenses through which AI and compute can be read, and AI is one of the tools through which Bataille can be re-read.

The output, when it exists, is a folder of insight notes that a person could pick up cold and use to think with — not a paper, not a book, not a survey. A working philosophical toolkit, current to now, forged in dialogue, with the agentic plumbing visible so the method itself is part of what's being built.
